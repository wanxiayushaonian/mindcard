Features

Everything you need to manage knowledge

From simple note-taking to AI-powered knowledge discovery

Markdown Cards

Rich text cards with Markdown support, emotion tags, keyword extraction, and smart linking between related ideas.

RAG AI Chat

AI conversations grounded in your knowledge base with source citations. Crystallize insights into new cards with one click.

Knowledge Topology

Automatic topic clustering with hierarchical knowledge trees. AI synthesizes related cards into structured insights.

Graph Visualization

Interactive D3.js force-directed graph showing card relationships. Timeline playback reveals knowledge evolution.

Multi-Model LLM

Switch between DeepSeek, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, and Moonshot in real-time. Unified provider architecture.

Team Collaboration

Workspace-based collaboration with four-tier permissions. Activity logs and notification system keep everyone in sync.

Conversation Fork

Fork any AI chat into a topology child node to explore sub-topics in depth. 4 branch profiles: Deep Dive, Explore, Summarize, and Challenge.

Workspace Memory

AI autonomously maintains persistent slug-keyed memory entries during chat. Memories inject into RAG context for personalized, continuity-aware responses.
